# amiLudo — identity exploration

Working name: amiLudo, combining game and shared play. Tagline: "All the tools. Around the table." Exact web search on October 30, 2026: no exact product identified; no domain or trademark availability guaranteed. Alternatives explored: Tournigo and amiLudo; Meeplio, Turnora and Tablyo ruled out because they are in use.

Audience: families, groups of friends, associations and regular players. Character: friendly, clear, tactile, reliable, open. Uses: mobile icon, web home page, GitHub repository. Decision maker: the project owner.

Avoid the generic cubic die, the die/timer/trophy stack and the standard meeple. One strong shape that reads at small sizes; black first. Palette considered after selection: ink #182C29 and lemon #D7EF71 on ivory #F6F4EC.

## Exploration: ten ideas

1. An open table where one player shifts aside to take their turn — abstract symbol.
2. An L whose foot becomes a shared board — monogram.
3. Two parentheses form a shared play space — abstract symbol.
4. Four tokens rotating around an empty center — circular symbol.
5. A loop interrupted by the passing of the baton — movement symbol.
6. The dot of the i becomes a player in a wordmark — typographic.
7. An open tile draws a doorway into the game — pictogram.
8. Two interlocking Ls draw two seats at the table — monogram.
9. A fan of rounds becomes a smile — abstract symbol.
10. A folded track forms a link between players — emblem.

## Selected logo: 12 — The box

A game box opens and a pawn springs out: all the tools are in the box, the game begins. The earlier tracks A, B and C are abandoned.

- Colors: purple #6644D6, yellow #F6C445, ink #2A1F44 for the monochrome version.
- Typography: headings in Bricolage Grotesque (free equivalent of Getai Grotesk Display), body text in Lil Grotesk.
- Files in design/logo: color and monochrome symbol, app icon (purple background, white box) and its maskable version. The Android, iOS and web icons are generated from these SVGs.
